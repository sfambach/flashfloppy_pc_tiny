# Mini Floppy Emulator – board spacers

3D-printable spacers that hold the cover board 11 mm above the main board
of the Mini Floppy Emulator. Two variants to try:

| Variant | Parts per post | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| A – screw spacer | `screw_spacer.stl` + 2 × M3 screws | Ø6 × 11 spacer with a Ø2.6 pilot bore; M3 screws from both sides cut their own thread. Countersunk M3×8 (DIN 7991) or button head M3×6 (ISO 7380). |
| B – snap pins | `snap_sleeve.stl` + 2 × `snap_pin.stl` | Ø6 × 11 sleeve with a 2 mm catch lip at each end; slotted pins go through the board holes from top and bottom and snap behind the lip. |

Drawings with all dimensions: `spacer-a-screw.svg`, `spacer-b-snap.svg`
(generated by `drawings.py`). Models: `spacer.scad`
(`openscad -D 'part="snap_pin"' -o snap_pin.stl spacer.scad`).

## Measured and assumed

- Measured: gap between the boards 11 mm, board holes about 3 mm.
- Assumed: board thickness 1.6 mm.
- A countersunk head cannot sink into a plain board hole; it stands about
  1.5 mm proud. A button head (1.65 mm) looks similar and is the fallback.
- An M3 screw is a tight fit in a 3.0 mm hole; drill to 3.2 mm if needed.
- Tolerances are set for FDM with a 0.4 mm nozzle; the snap pin is small,
  so print it head down in PETG and adjust `barb_d`/`lip_d` if it is too
  tight or too loose.

Not printed or tested yet.
